CLAUSE 1. POSITION AND PLACE OF WORK

The Employee agrees to work in the position of position, based at location/branch, country, reporting to supervisor/position. The Employee shall devote full working time to the Employer and shall not engage in any other occupation or employment without the Employer's prior written consent.

CLAUSE 2. TERM AND PROBATION

This Contract is for a term of __ year(s), from __/__/____ to __/__/____.

The probation period is __ month(s). During probation the Employer shall pay ____ per month (or ____ per day). Upon passing the criteria in Annex A, the Employee shall receive the full rate. The Employer shall give written notice of the probation outcome no less than 7 days before the end of probation.

CLAUSE 3. REMUNERATION AND PAYMENT

Salary: amount in figures and words. Currency actually paid: ____. Local allowance: ____ per month, currency ____.

Payment shall be made on the __ day of each month to account bank / account no. / account name. Where payment is in a different currency, the reference exchange rate of the central bank of the country of work on the transfer date shall apply. Remuneration shall not be less than the statutory minimum wage.

CLAUSE 4. WORKING HOURS, REST DAYS AND OVERTIME

Normal working hours shall not exceed 8 hours per day and 48 hours per week. Weekly rest day(s): __. Public and traditional holidays of the country of work: not fewer than __ days per year.

Overtime requires prior written approval and shall be paid at not less than the statutory rates: 150% of the hourly rate on normal working days and 200% on weekly rest days / public holidays (or such higher rate as the law of that country requires).

CLAUSE 5. LEAVE

The Employee is entitled to paid leave as prescribed by law, including at minimum: annual leave __ days; sick leave __ days; personal leave __ days; maternity / spouse maternity support leave __ days; other statutory leave __ days. Details are set out in Annex B. The phrase "as necessary" shall not be used.

CLAUSE 6. SOCIAL SECURITY

The Employer shall register the Employee with the social security authority of the country of work and remit contributions at the statutory rates for both employer and employee, provide the Employee with proof of remittance, and inform the Employee of the social security registration number.

CLAUSE 7. PERSONAL INCOME TAX

Remuneration under this Contract is subject to personal income tax under the law of the country of work. The Employer shall withhold and remit tax to the tax authority within the statutory deadlines and issue the Employee a withholding tax certificate. Any applicable double taxation agreement shall apply.

CLAUSE 8. VISA, WORK PERMIT AND IMMIGRATION DOCUMENTS

The Employer is responsible for obtaining, at its own cost, the work visa and Work Permit lawfully required, and for renewing them throughout the term. The Employee shall cooperate by providing documents and truthful information.

If the visa or work permit is refused or revoked without fault of the Employee, the Employer shall remedy the matter promptly and place the Employee on paid standby. If it cannot be remedied within __ days, this Contract shall terminate and the Employer shall pay severance and return travel costs under Clause 13.

The Employee shall not commence work before a valid Work Permit is issued. Breach is a serious offence.

CLAUSE 9. ACCOMMODATION, MEALS, TRAVEL AND BENEFITS

The Employer shall provide accommodation to a reasonable standard per Annex D, free of rent. The Employer remains liable for loss or injury caused by accommodation that is unsafe or below standard.

The Employer shall bear travel costs between countries at the start and end of the Contract, including __ annual leave air/transport tickets per year, as detailed in Annex D.

CLAUSE 10. MEDICAL EXPENSES, HEALTH AND ACCIDENT INSURANCE

The Employer shall provide health and accident insurance with coverage of not less than ____ per occurrence per Annex E, covering ordinary illness in addition to work-related injury.

In the event of serious illness or injury that cannot be treated in that country, the Employer shall bear the cost of repatriation / medical evacuation. In the event of death, the Employer shall bear the reasonable cost of returning the remains to the home country.

CLAUSE 11. TERMINATION

This Contract terminates upon: (1) expiry of the term; (2) death of the Employee; (3) termination by either party with no less than 30 days' prior written notice, or earlier upon payment in lieu of notice; (4) termination by the Employer where a government-certified physician certifies that the Employee is medically unfit to continue; (5) termination by the Employer for serious misconduct under Clause 12.

CLAUSE 12. DISCIPLINE, WORK RULES AND PENALTIES

Disciplinary action shall be taken progressively: written warning → probation/suspension → partial deduction of wages → dismissal, and the Employee shall be given an opportunity to explain before any sanction at each level.

"Serious misconduct" permitting immediate termination includes: fraud, embezzlement, theft, wilful damage to property, absence from duty for 3 consecutive working days without reasonable cause, disclosure of confidential information, giving or receiving improper benefits, working without a valid work permit.

CLAUSE 13. SEVERANCE AND GRATUITY ON TERMINATION

(1) On termination under Clause 11 (1)(2)(3)(4), the Employer shall pay severance in accordance with the labour law of the country of work at the rates in force on the termination date, per Annex F.

(2) Where termination results from refusal or revocation of a visa / work permit without fault of the Employee, the Employer shall pay severance and return travel costs per Annex F.

(3) Where termination results from serious misconduct under Clause 12, the Employee is not entitled to severance to the extent excluded by law, but shall receive wages up to the termination date and return travel costs under Clause 9.

(4) Payment shall be made within __ days of the termination date, together with a certificate of employment.

CLAUSE 14. HANDOVER AND EMPLOYER PROPERTY

Before the termination date, the Employee shall return all work, documents, data and Employer property in their possession, and the Employer shall issue a certificate of employment and a withholding tax certificate.

CLAUSE 15. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Employee shall keep confidential all information of the Employer, customers, suppliers and business obtained during employment, throughout the term and for a further __ year(s) after termination, in particular customer/debtor data, financial data, prices, costs and business methods.

CLAUSE 16. NON-COMPETITION AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST

During the term and for __ year(s) thereafter, the Employee shall not engage in or join a business competing with the Employer within the business scope of ____, and shall disclose conflicts of interest in accordance with the Employer's policy.

CLAUSE 17. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND EMPLOYER DATA

All work, data, documents or materials created by the Employee in the course of employment are the property of the Employer. The Employee shall not copy, use or disclose them without authorisation.

CLAUSE 18. PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION

The Employer shall collect, use and disclose the Employee's personal data only for employment, payroll, social security, tax and legal compliance purposes, in accordance with the personal data protection law of the country of work and of Thailand. The Employee has the right to access, correct or obtain a copy of their data. Cross-border transfers shall be made only where necessary and with a lawful basis.

CLAUSE 19. OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY, HEALTH AND WORK ENVIRONMENT

The Employer shall provide a safe working environment, safety training, personal protective equipment, and shall report accidents in accordance with the law. The Employee shall strictly observe all safety rules.

CLAUSE 20. AMENDMENT AND NOTICES

Any amendment shall be in writing and signed by both parties. Notices shall be sent to address / email of each party and are deemed received __ day(s) after dispatch.

CLAUSE 21. FORCE MAJEURE

If a force majeure event (natural disaster, epidemic, civil unrest, government measure or other event beyond control) prevents performance, the parties shall negotiate a solution and the Employer shall bear the Employee's return travel costs.

CLAUSE 22. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Disputes arising from this Contract shall first be resolved by negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute shall be submitted to the labour authority or arbitration body of the country of work, or to the competent court of that country.

CLAUSE 23. GOVERNING LAW

This Contract is governed by the laws of the country where the Employee works, together with the Employer's internal work rules to the extent not inconsistent with those laws.

CLAUSE 24. LANGUAGES AND COPIES

This Contract is executed in four languages: Lao, Khmer, Thai and English, with identical clauses. Each party retains one set. In the event of conflict, the version in the language of the country where the Employee works shall prevail.
