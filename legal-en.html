📌 Company

Auto Guarantee Co., Ltd. / AAM Capital Service Co., Ltd. / RPTN Allianz Fintech Co., Ltd. / Ruam Pattana Leasing Co., Ltd. and affiliated companies (hereinafter referred to as the "Company")

places great importance on the protection of personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019). Therefore, to enable the Company to collect, use, or disclose personal data in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), the Company hereby requests consent from job applicants, as data subjects, to collect, use, or disclose their personal data with the following details. The applicant acknowledges that this consent form is part of the job application.

📝 Consent

I acknowledge that I have received and understood the Company's Personal Data Protection Policy regarding the collection, use, or disclosure of my personal data for the purposes described above, and hereby give my consent to the Company as follows:

✅ I consent to the collection, use, disclosure, and transfer of my personal data as detailed in this consent form.

📋 Sections 1–7 Details

Section 1. Purpose of Collection, Use, or Disclosure of Personal Data

The Company collects, uses, and discloses personal data of job applicants for the following purposes:

  • To carry out the Company's recruitment process.
  • To assess whether your qualifications and skills are suitable for the position.
  • To facilitate communication, including scheduling job interviews and sending news related to job positions.
  • To verify the applicant's identity and the accuracy of personal data and/or other information provided to the Company.
  • To share personal data and/or other information of the applicant with the Company, both in Thailand and abroad.
  • To comply with applicable laws, such as labor laws, tax laws, and any other laws or regulations applicable to the Company.

Section 2. Types of Personal Data

The personal data collected, used, or disclosed by the Company includes the following details:

  • First name, last name, nickname, gender, photograph, date of birth.
  • Resume, Curriculum Vitae (CV), cover letter, work experience, and educational background.
  • Contact information such as address, email address, phone number, etc.
  • Professional licenses or other work-related licenses, certificates, or qualifications held.
  • IP Address information or data collected through Cookies or similar technologies.
  • Personal data of other individuals, such as emergency contacts, references, beneficiaries, details or documents regarding spouse, children, parents, siblings, and family members, including phone/mobile numbers of such individuals. By providing third-party personal data to the Company, you certify and warrant that you have the authority to do so and permit the Company to use such personal data in accordance with this notice. You are also responsible for informing such individuals of this notice and/or obtaining their consent.
  • Information collected from applicants, such as information provided during job interviews, test results, and images and/or audio recordings captured during recruitment-related activities.

Section 3. Retention Period of Personal Data

The Company will retain your personal data for a period of 2 years from the conclusion of the final interview, except in the following cases:

  • If the applicant is selected for employment, the Company will continue to retain your personal data for as long as necessary to achieve the Company's purposes.
  • If there is a legitimate request from the data subject, a government agency order, or a court order.
  • Contact information such as address, email address, phone number, etc.

Section 4. Disclosure of Personal Data

The Company will not disclose such personal data to any person without the data subject's consent. However, for the purpose of carrying out the objectives of collection, use, or disclosure as stated above, the applicant acknowledges and agrees that the Company may disclose personal data to the following parties:

  • The Company, both in Thailand and abroad.
  • Individuals or organizations engaged by the Company to perform tasks related to personal data for the benefit of the Company's management, such as IT service providers, data analytics providers, statistical analysis, research and product development, design, etc.
  • The Company may disclose personal data of applicants as required by law, such as disclosure to government agencies, public organizations, labor welfare regulatory bodies, including cases where disclosure is requested under legal authority, such as requests for information for litigation or legal proceedings, enforcement of judgments, etc.

Section 5. Rights of the Applicant as a Data Subject

The applicant, as a data subject, has the following rights:

  • To access and request a copy of personal data held by the Company, or to request the Company to disclose the source of personal data that was obtained without consent.
  • To request the Company to correct, update, or complete personal data to ensure accuracy and prevent misunderstanding.
  • To object to the collection, use, or disclosure of personal data where the law permits processing without consent, at any time.
  • To request the Company to delete, destroy, or anonymize personal data in circumstances prescribed by law.
  • To request the Company to suspend the use of personal data in circumstances prescribed by law.
  • To withdraw consent at any time.
  • To lodge a complaint if the Company or any data processor, including employees or contractors of the Company, violates or fails to comply with the Personal Data Protection Act.

Section 6. Effect of Withdrawal of Consent

The consent provided by the applicant for the collection, use, or disclosure of personal data for the purposes stated above shall remain in effect until the applicant withdraws consent in writing to the Company. Upon receiving the applicant's request as specified in Section 5, the Company will review and inform the applicant of any potential impact of such request (if any) and will complete the request within 30 days from the date of receiving the written request in the form prescribed by the Company. The withdrawal of consent shall not affect the collection, use, or disclosure of personal data that the applicant had previously consented to.

Section 7. Contact Information

Applicants may contact the Human Resource Effectiveness Management Department or the Data Protection Officer through the following channels:

  • Email: siriwanson@prachakij.com
  • By mail or in person to: Human Resources Department (Ms. Siriwanson Songsri) or the Data Protection Officer.
  • Address: Auto Guarantee Co., Ltd. 50/11 Moo 2, Tha Chang Subdistrict, Mueang District, Chanthaburi Province 22000.
